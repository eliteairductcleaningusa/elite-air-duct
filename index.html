Is Your Home Making You Sick?

The EPA notes that indoor air can hold several times more of some pollutants than outdoor air. Dirty ducts and vents can add to the problem.

🤧Dust & Allergens

Dust, pet dander and pollen build up in ducts and get pushed back into your rooms.

🦠Mold & Musty Smells

Damp, dirty ducts can encourage mold growth and stale odors throughout your home.

🔥Fire Hazards

Lint in dryer vents and soot in chimneys are well-known causes of house fires.

Warning Signs You Shouldn't Ignore

If you notice any of these, it's time for a professional cleaning.

Dryer Vent

  • Clothes take more than one cycle to dry
  • Dryer is very hot on the outside
  • Burning smell while drying
  • Lint around the door or vent outlet
  • Vent not cleaned in over a year
Book Dryer Vent Cleaning

Chimney

  • Smoky smell or smoke blowing into the room
  • Black soot buildup you can see
  • Dark, flaky creosote on the walls
  • Fire burns poorly or draws badly
  • Not cleaned in over a year
Book Chimney Cleaning

How often should I clean my ducts, dryer vent and chimney?

Common guidance is every few years for air ducts, about once a year for dryer vents, and annually for chimneys that are used regularly. Heavy use, pets, allergies or visible buildup can mean sooner.
